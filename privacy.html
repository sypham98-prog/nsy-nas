Privacy policy

NSY NAS runs only on the owner's home router and is used only by the owner and their household.

  • Google Drive access is used solely to show the owner's own Drive files as a network drive on the owner's home network.
  • The access token is stored only on the owner's router. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • Files are cached temporarily in the router's memory to make opening them faster, and are not kept elsewhere.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact: via the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
