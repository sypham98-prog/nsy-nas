NSY NAS

Personal home network drive · Ổ mạng gia đình cá nhân

NSY NAS is a private application that runs only on the owner's home router (NSY-G68). It mounts the owner's own Google Drive accounts with rclone and shares them as a password-protected network drive inside the owner's home network.

It is not a public service: it is used only by the owner and their household, and no one else can sign in to it.
